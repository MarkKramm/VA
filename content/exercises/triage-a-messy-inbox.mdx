#### The inbox

Fifteen messages, in arrival order. Do not open anything — decide from what you can
see, which is what triage actually is.

| #   | From           | Subject                 | First two lines                                                              |
| --- | -------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Client         | Re: Friday's meeting    | "Thursday 14:00 works for me."                                               |
| 2   | Supplier       | Your invoice 4471       | "Please find attached our invoice for March."                                |
| 3   | Client         | The March report        | "Can you put together the March numbers before the board meeting?"           |
| 4   | Newsletter     | Weekly design digest    | "This week: 12 new templates."                                               |
| 5   | Client         | URGENT                  | "Need the contract by end of day. It's the one we discussed."                |
| 6   | Customer       | Refund request          | "I was charged twice for the same order."                                    |
| 7   | Colleague      | Lunch?                  | "Are you around at 12?"                                                      |
| 8   | Client         | Invoice query           | "Why is line 3 higher than last month?"                                      |
| 9   | Notification   | Calendar reminder       | "Standing meeting in 15 minutes."                                            |
| 10  | Client         | New project             | "We're starting with a new supplier next month — can you set up the folder?" |
| 11  | Bank           | Statement ready         | "Your statement is available."                                               |
| 12  | Client         | Following up            | "Any news on the supplier statement I asked about last week?"                |
| 13  | Unknown sender | Partnership opportunity | "Dear sir/madam, we would like to discuss a business opportunity."           |
| 14  | Client         | Wrong file              | "I think you sent me last month's version."                                  |
| 15  | Supplier       | Out of office           | "I am away until Monday."                                                    |

#### What to produce

For each message, one line:

```text
1  Do now      — confirm the time, one line reply
3  Task        — needs research; due before the board meeting (date?)
5  Ask         — which contract? cannot start without it
13 Delete/flag — unknown sender, generic greeting, no specifics
```

Then a follow-up list for anything where the ball is in someone else's court.

#### How it will be judged

- **Nothing undecided.** Fifteen lines, fifteen decisions.
- **Message 5 is a trap.** "URGENT" plus "the one we discussed" is an ambiguous
  request wearing a deadline. The right decision is to ask, quickly.
- **Message 12 outranks message 3**, because you already owe an answer.
- **Message 13 is a decision, not a deletion.** An unknown sender with a generic
  greeting and no specifics is a pattern you should be able to name.
- **Message 2 is not a task.** It is a file you may need later.
