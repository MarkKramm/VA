#### The batch

Create twenty records with these columns. Deliberately make the source slightly
awkward — mix the formats, as real sources do.

```text
Name | Company | Email | Phone | Date joined | Amount
```

Copy them from anywhere: a web page, a document, a list you write yourself in a
scrambled order. The source does not matter; the verification does.

#### What to produce

**1. The entered data**, in a spreadsheet.

**2. The verification record:**

```text
Source rows:      20
Entered rows:     20
Direction:        entered down the sheet, verified across each row
Spot-checked:     first row, last row, longest company name, smallest amount
Totals:           Amount column sums to <x> in both source and destination
```

**3. The entry log**, naming anything you could not enter cleanly and why.

#### How it will be judged

- **A row count, twice.** Without it you have no evidence you finished.
- **A different verification method.** Reading back what you typed does not count,
  because you read what you meant.
- **Transposition is the enemy.** Swap two digits in one phone number on purpose,
  then see whether your verification catches it. If it does not, change your
  method.
- **The log names the gaps.** A record with no email, an unreadable date, a phone
  number in a format you were not sure about — each one is a line, not a silent
  omission.
