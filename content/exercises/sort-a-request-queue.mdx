#### The queue

| #   | Request                                         | Notes                                      |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Confirm Thursday's meeting                      | You have the client's availability         |
| 2   | Send the signed contract to the supplier        | You have the signed PDF                    |
| 3   | Prepare the monthly summary                     | Needs last month's figures, which you have |
| 4   | Update the client's contact list                | You do not know which list is current      |
| 5   | Reply to a customer asking about delivery times | You do not know the delivery times         |
| 6   | Book a venue for the team day                   | Date not confirmed                         |
| 7   | Fix the typo on the pricing page                | You have edit access                       |
| 8   | Research three competitors                      | Substantial work, no deadline given        |
| 9   | Send the invoice for this month                 | Waiting on the client to confirm hours     |
| 10  | Archive last year's project folders             | Nobody is waiting                          |
| 11  | Draft a reply to a complaint                    | Needs a decision on the refund             |
| 12  | Add a new starter to the shared drive           | You have their name and email              |
| 13  | Update the team on progress                     | You have nothing new since yesterday       |
| 14  | Renew the domain before it expires              | Expires in two days                        |

#### What to produce

```text
Do now (minutes each)
  1  Confirm Thursday   — one line reply, nothing needed from anyone
  7  Fix the typo       — have access, takes two minutes
  …

Ask (blocked on a detail)
  4  Which contact list?     5  What are the delivery times?
  …

Work (scheduled)
  3  Monthly summary   — needs a block, you have the inputs
  …

Defer (with a date)
  8  Competitor research — nothing is waiting, schedule for next week
  10 Archive folders     — low value, do it when the queue is empty
```

Then the batched question message, and the closing status.

#### How it will be judged

- **#14 is first, not last.** A domain expiring in two days is a hard deadline with
  a consequence nobody can undo. It is the item most likely to be deferred because
  it is boring.
- **#9 is "ask", not "work".** You cannot invoice hours the client has not
  confirmed.
- **#13 is neither.** An update with nothing new is noise, and sending it trains
  the client to ignore updates.
- **#11 is not yours to answer.** A refund decision is an escalation.
- **Four questions in one message**, not four messages.
