#### The requests

Work through all five. Write your answer for each before reading the next.

1. "Is there anything official about how long we have to keep invoices?"
2. "Who is the current CEO of that packaging company in Rotterdam?"
3. "What does the error `E_ACCESSDENIED` mean when we upload?"
4. "Find me two other companies doing what we do."
5. "What's the standard notice period for cancelling a contract like ours?"

#### What to produce

For each request, write four lines:

```text
Request 1
Query:        invoice retention period
Operator:     site:gov.uk invoice retention
Trusted:      the tax authority's own guidance
Why:          it is the body that sets the rule, not a page describing it
```

#### How it will be judged

- The query is built from **nouns**, not the client's sentence.
- At least two of the five use an operator (`site:`, quotes, or a minus).
- The "trusted" line names a **kind of source** and explains why that source
  would know. "A good website" is not an answer.
- Where the request is ambiguous — request 4 and request 5 both are — you say what
  you would ask before searching.
