#### The day

You have a seven-hour working day. Eleven things want your attention.

| #   | From     | Request                                        | Due        |
| --- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1   | Client A | Send March figures so they can invoice         | today      |
| 2   | Client B | "Quick" question about last month's report     | not stated |
| 3   | Client A | Prepare slides for Friday's board meeting      | Friday     |
| 4   | Client C | Set up a folder for a new project              | next week  |
| 5   | Client B | Chase a supplier who has not replied           | not stated |
| 6   | Internal | Update the invoice template with a new logo    | not stated |
| 7   | Client C | Confirm tomorrow's meeting                     | today      |
| 8   | Client A | "Can we talk about the contract?"              | not stated |
| 9   | Client B | Clean a 400-row contact list                   | not stated |
| 10  | Client C | A message that is three tasks in one paragraph | not stated |
| 11  | You      | The task you have been avoiding for a week     | overdue    |

#### What to produce

**1. The ranked list**, with the consequence named:

```text
1. #1 Send March figures      — blocks invoicing; someone cannot bill without it
2. #7 Confirm tomorrow's mtg  — a meeting that fails costs everyone the slot
3. …
```

**2. The day plan** — blocks, not hours, and no more than two thirds of the day
filled.

**3. The conflict message** to whichever client is getting less than they hoped.

#### How it will be judged

- **#1 is first.** Someone cannot get paid without it.
- **#2 is not urgent.** "Quick question" is a description of how it feels to ask,
  not of the work.
- **#11 gets scheduled.** The task you have been avoiding is usually the one that
  is blocking something else, and putting it last is how it becomes overdue again.
- **The unplanned third is visible in your plan.** A day with no slack is a plan
  you already know will fail.
- **The conflict message proposes a time.** "Sorry, I couldn't get to this" is not
  a plan.
