## Why this roadmap exists

Non-voice work is not a fallback for people who dislike calls. It is a large part
of the remote market, because asynchronous work is written work — an inbox, a
ticket queue, a research brief and a spreadsheet are all handled in text, and a
client who needs cover across several time zones hires for writing on purpose.

## What it covers

The first three stages are the shared foundation: what the work is, how to use a
computer well, and how to write to a client. They are the same modules the
Beginner VA and Data Entry VA roadmaps use, referenced by id rather than copied.

From there this roadmap goes in one direction: files, data, documents and
research, then a module on running a written-only working day. The capstone is a
practical assessment in which you organise a client's digital workspace and hand
over a written note explaining what you did.

## What it does not cover

Specialisations — social media, bookkeeping, project coordination — belong to
later roadmaps. This one establishes the written foundation they all build on.
