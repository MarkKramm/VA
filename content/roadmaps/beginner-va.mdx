## Where this roadmap starts

You do not need any experience to begin. You do need to be willing to be
inexpert in public for a while, which is the part most people find harder than
the work itself.

This roadmap does two things. It gives you an accurate picture of the role, so
that your expectations match reality. And it gives you the small set of working
habits — files, naming, browser organisation — that separate someone who looks
professional within a week from someone who still looks like they are guessing.

It deliberately does not take you all the way to employable. That is what the
specialisation roadmaps are for, and choosing between them is a decision you are
better placed to make once you understand the field.
