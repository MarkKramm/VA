## About data entry

Data entry is often described as the entry-level corner of this industry, and
that is half true. The work is approachable, it is a genuine on-ramp, and it
will build habits that transfer into almost every other VA specialisation.

The other half is that unstructured data entry is being automated, and the
entry-level work that is growing is the _careful_ kind: cleaning, reconciling,
validating, and catching what the source got wrong. That is more interesting
work than typing rows, and it is the version worth aiming for.

The spreadsheet and data-cleaning modules arrive at M2. This roadmap exists now
because the architecture needs more than one consumer of a shared module, and
the honest way to prove that is to write a second roadmap that genuinely shares
content rather than asserting that sharing would work.
