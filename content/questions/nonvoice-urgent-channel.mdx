prompt: >-
  A written-only working arrangement does not need an agreed channel for genuine
  emergencies, because everything can be handled in writing.
answer: false
explanation: >-
  A written-only arrangement absolutely needs one: without an agreed signal for
  something genuinely urgent, either every problem is escalated as an emergency or
  a real one waits until your next pass. The agreement should name the channel and
  the shorter response window, and ideally say what counts as urgent. That is what
  keeps the arrangement workable rather than a permanent source of anxiety for
  both sides.
