---
id: data-verification-method
type: single-choice
prompt: >-
  You have just entered two hundred phone numbers down a column. Which
  verification method is most likely to catch a transposed pair of digits?
choices:
  - id: read-back
    text: Read the column back from the top, slowly
  - id: cross-check
    text: Check across each row instead of down the column
  - id: sort
    text: Sort the column and check it looks right
  - id: count
    text: Count the rows to confirm all two hundred are present
correctChoiceId: cross-check
explanation: >-
  Reading back what you typed does not work, because you read what you meant.
  Changing direction forces your eye to process the data rather than the pattern,
  which is what catches a transposition. A row count is a useful check but it
  proves completeness, not accuracy — two hundred wrong numbers still count as two
  hundred.
