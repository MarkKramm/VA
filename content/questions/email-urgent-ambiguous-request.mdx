id: email-urgent-ambiguous-request
type: single-choice
prompt: >-
  A client emails with the subject "URGENT" and the message "Need the contract by
  end of day. It's the one we discussed." What should you do first?
choices:
  - id: search-and-send
    text: Search for the most recent contract and send it
  - id: ask-which
    text: Ask which contract they mean, and reply quickly
  - id: wait
    text: Wait until you can find one that matches, then send it
  - id: ignore
    text: Treat it as low priority because the request is vague
correctChoiceId: ask-which
explanation: >-
  "The one we discussed" is an ambiguous request wearing a deadline. Sending the
  wrong contract under time pressure is worse than asking a one-line question, and
  the question takes seconds. Treating it as low priority is also wrong: the
  deadline may be real, which is exactly why the ambiguity has to be resolved
  immediately rather than guessed at.
