prompt: >-
  A client asks for the current registered address of a company in another
  country. Which source should you trust most?
choices:
  - id: a-news-article
    text: A news article about the company from two years ago
  - id: company-website
    text: The address on the company's own website
  - id: official-registry
    text: The company register published by that country's government
  - id: business-directory
    text: A business directory site that lists thousands of companies
correctChoiceId: official-registry
explanation: >-
  A registry is a primary source: the body that holds the record. The company's
  own website is a close second and usually right, but it is the company
  describing itself, and a stale "contact us" page is common. A news article is a
  snapshot from a date, and directory sites copy from each other, so agreement
  between two of them proves nothing.
