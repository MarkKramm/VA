id: nonvoice-batching
type: single-choice
prompt: >-
  In a written-support role, why is answering messages in two or three scheduled
  passes a day usually better than answering them as they arrive?
choices:
  - id: total-faster
    text: It is faster in total and interrupts the work the messages are about
  - id: looks-busy
    text: It makes the client think you are busier than you are
  - id: platform
    text: Most platforms require it
  - id: avoids-replies
    text: It reduces the number of messages you have to answer
correctChoiceId: total-faster
explanation: >-
  Every arriving message interrupts the work it is about, and switching between
  them costs more than the messages take. Batching means the substantive work gets
  a block of concentration, and the replies are still sent within the response time
  you agreed. It does not reduce the number of messages, and it should never be
  used to look busy.
