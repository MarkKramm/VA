prompt: >-
  A client sends you a spreadsheet containing their customers’ contact details. What is the
  right first step?
choices:
  - id: confirm-what-is-expected
    text: Confirm what the client wants done with it, and where the finished file should live
  - id: share-with-a-friend
    text: Share it with a friend who is good with spreadsheets
  - id: upload-publicly
    text: Upload it to a public file-sharing link
  - id: delete-it
    text: Delete it, because handling customer data is not allowed
correctChoiceId: confirm-what-is-expected
explanation: >-
  Client data is handled only for the task it was sent for, and the first step is agreeing
  what that task is. Sharing it, publishing it, or deleting it unasked all break that
  agreement.
