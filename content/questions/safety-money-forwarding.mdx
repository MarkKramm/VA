prompt: >-
  A client offers you a paid role described as "payment processing": money arrives
  in your bank account and you forward it on to suppliers, keeping a small
  commission. What is the main risk to you?
choices:
  - id: admin-burden
    text: The record-keeping would be time-consuming
  - id: commission
    text: The commission is probably too small for the work
  - id: money-laundering
    text: You would be handling criminal proceeds, in your own name
  - id: no-risk
    text: None, as long as you keep clear records of every transfer
correctChoiceId: money-laundering
explanation: >-
  Receiving and forwarding money through your own account is money laundering, and
  it makes you the person the bank and the authorities investigate. Keeping
  records does not help: the records show that you did it. No legitimate employer
  needs an individual's personal bank account to move its money.
