type: single-choice
prompt: >-
  You have finished three of four tasks for a client and the fourth needs a
  decision from them. What should the first line of your message say?
choices:
  - id: what-is-blocked
    text: That three are done and the fourth needs a decision
  - id: context
    text: A reminder of when the tasks were sent and how long you have worked on them
  - id: apology
    text: An apology for not finishing all four
  - id: greeting
    text: A friendly opening paragraph before the update
correctChoiceId: what-is-blocked
explanation: >-
  The client needs to know what to do. Leading with what is finished and what is
  blocked lets them reply in one line. An apology for something that is not your
  fault sets the wrong expectation, and context before content makes them read to
  the end to find out whether anything is wrong.
