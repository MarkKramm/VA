  A prospective client offers you a well-paid administrative role, then asks you to
  pay for an onboarding and training package which they say is refunded with your
  first payment. What does this most likely indicate?
choices:
  - id: normal
    text: A normal arrangement for a large company
  - id: advance-fee-fraud
    text: An advance-fee fraud — real employers pay you, they do not charge you
  - id: negotiate
    text: A legitimate fee you could negotiate down
  - id: paperwork
    text: A paperwork step you should complete before signing a contract
correctChoiceId: advance-fee-fraud
explanation: >-
  Charging someone for a job is the defining feature of advance-fee fraud, and the
  promise of a refund is what makes it feel safe. There is no legitimate
  arrangement in which a new employer requires a payment from the person they are
  hiring. The refund never arrives, and the payment details you supplied are used
  again.
