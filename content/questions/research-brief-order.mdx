type: single-choice
prompt: >-
  You have spent an hour on a research question and are writing it up. What
  belongs in the first line of the brief?
choices:
  - id: the-answer
    text: The finding itself, in one or two sentences
  - id: the-method
    text: A summary of where you searched and how long it took
  - id: the-caveat
    text: A note that some sources disagreed
  - id: the-greeting
    text: A short friendly opening before the findings
correctChoiceId: the-answer
explanation: >-
  The client's first question is "what did you find", not "how did you get there".
  Method and caveats belong in the brief, but underneath the answer. Leading with
  the process makes the reader do the work you were hired to do.
