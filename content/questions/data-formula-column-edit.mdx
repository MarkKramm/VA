type: single-choice
prompt: >-
  A client's sheet has a total in the last row that is calculated by a formula. You
  need to add ten rows of data above it. What should you check afterwards?
choices:
  - id: total-changed
    text: That the total changed by the amount you added
  - id: formatting
    text: That the new rows match the formatting of the old ones
  - id: column-width
    text: That the columns are still wide enough to read
  - id: freeze-panes
    text: That the header row is still frozen
correctChoiceId: total-changed
explanation: >-
  A formula covers a fixed range. If the new rows fall outside it, the total
  silently stays wrong — and it looks correct, which is worse than an error. Adding
  rows rather than pasting over the data usually extends the range, but checking
  the arithmetic is what proves it did. Formatting and layout matter; a wrong total
  is the one that gets acted on.
