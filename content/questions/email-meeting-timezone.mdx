prompt: >-
  A meeting invitation should state the time zone, or the time in a form that
  cannot be misread.
answer: true
explanation: >-
  "Thursday 15:00" with no zone is a guess, and a guess that half the attendees
  will make differently. Time zones also shift twice a year on different dates in
  different countries, so a meeting that worked all winter can silently move. Naming
  the zone, or giving two reference points such as the client's time and UTC,
  removes the ambiguity entirely.
