id: research-independent-sources
type: true-false
prompt: >-
  Two articles that both repeat the same press release count as two independent
  sources, so the claim can be treated as confirmed.
answer: false
explanation: >-
  They are one source quoted twice. Independence means the second source reached
  the conclusion without the first — two reports of the same press release, or two
  directory sites that copied each other, confirm nothing. To confirm a claim you
  need a primary source, or two sources that genuinely did not rely on each other.
