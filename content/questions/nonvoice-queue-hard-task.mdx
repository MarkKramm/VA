id: nonvoice-queue-hard-task
type: single-choice
prompt: >-
  You have a queue of twenty written requests and one working session. What is the
  most common failure in a session like this?
choices:
  - id: easy-first
    text: Clearing the quick items repeatedly and never starting the substantial ones
  - id: asking
    text: Sending the client a batch of questions
  - id: deferring
    text: Writing down what you are deferring with a date
  - id: one-at-a-time
    text: Finishing one item before opening the next
correctChoiceId: easy-first
explanation: >-
  The easy items are satisfying and there is always another one, so a session can
  end with twenty small things done and none of the work the client actually asked
  for. The other three are all good practice: batching questions respects the
  client's time, writing down deferrals is how you avoid forgetting them, and
  finishing one item at a time is what stops you reopening things.
