Almost every virtual assistant role is, at its core, a computer role. The
software changes from client to client, but the underlying skills — finding a
file, keeping a folder navigable, not losing a download — transfer directly.

## What this module deliberately leaves out

It does not teach a specific operating system in depth, and it does not cover
typing speed or shortcut memorisation. Those are learnable by doing and are
secondary to the habits below. The habits are the part that transfers, and they
are the part clients notice.
