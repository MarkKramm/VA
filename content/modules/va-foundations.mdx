Most people arrive at virtual assistance with a vague idea of the title and no
clear picture of the work. This module fixes that before anything practical
begins, because almost every early mistake — bad expectations, poor client
communication, agreeing to the wrong kind of engagement — traces back to not
knowing what the role actually involves.

## Why this module comes first

The rest of the platform assumes you know what a VA is. If you skip this, the
later material will technically make sense and practically not land, because
each lesson refers to clients, tasks and expectations that have not been
introduced.

Read the two lessons in order. They are short by design: this is orientation,
not a foundation course in virtual assistance.
